European Time Zones Overview

European continent is divided into several standard time zones. Knowing the exact offset is essential for international business, travel, and virtual meetings across different countries.

🌐 Western European Time (WET / GMT)

Covers Western Europe, including the UK, Ireland, Portugal, and the Faroe Islands. During summer, these countries observe WEST (UTC+1) or BST (British Summer Time).

🏛️ Central European Time (CET)

The most widely used timezone in Europe (UTC+1). Spans France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Poland, Switzerland, Austria, and Scandinavia. Daylight Saving Time operates at CEST (UTC+2).

🏛️ Eastern European Time (EET)

Standard time zone (UTC+2) used in Greece, Finland, Ukraine, Romania, Bulgaria, Estonia, Latvia, and Lithuania. During Daylight Saving Time, offsets move to EEST (UTC+3).

🕌 Moscow Time (MSK)

Covers western parts of Russia including Moscow and Saint Petersburg (UTC+3). Russia does not currently observe Daylight Saving Time.
